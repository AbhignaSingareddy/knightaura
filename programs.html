— GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before your first move?

Find answers to common questions before choosing your chess learning path.

Which program should a beginner choose?

The Foundation track is a useful starting point if you are learning piece movement, rules, basic tactics and how to play a complete game.

Can I join if I already know the basics?

Yes. The Developing Player track focuses on calculation, planning, positional understanding and game review.

How can I prepare for tournaments?

The Tournament Track focuses on practical preparation, timed calculation, endgames and reviewing competitive games.

How do I choose my skill level?

Consider your knowledge of the rules, your playing experience and the areas you want to improve. Contact the academy to discuss a suitable learning path.

How can I enquire about joining?

Visit the Contact page and share your chess experience and learning goals to explore a suitable program.

How often should I practise chess?

Regular practice is more effective than occasional long sessions. Start with short, focused sessions and gradually improve your calculation, tactics and game analysis as you progress.

— GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions before game day.

Not sure where to begin? These answers explain what to ask before joining an event.

Still have a question?
Can beginners participate in tournaments?

Some friendly events may welcome beginners, while others may have specific eligibility criteria. Ask the academy which event best suits your current experience.

How do I know the tournament date?

Contact the academy for confirmed dates, timings, venue, registration deadlines and any participation requirements.

What should I bring on tournament day?

Follow the organiser's instructions. Arrive on time and bring any requested identification or equipment. Confirm whether chess sets and clocks will be provided.

Do I need previous tournament experience?

Not necessarily. The right event depends on its format and eligibility rules. Ask the academy about suitable options for your experience level.

Is this form a confirmed tournament booking?

No. This form is an enquiry interface. Your participation is not confirmed until the academy provides the official event details and registration confirmation.

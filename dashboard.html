COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which batch should I choose as a beginner?

Chess Foundations is intended for learners who are new to chess. If you already know the rules, ask the academy which level best matches your current skills.

Does selecting a batch confirm my admission?

No. Selection records your interest in this dashboard demo. The academy must confirm seat availability and admission separately.

How can I find the actual fees and timings?

Contact the academy to confirm current fees, schedules, course duration, and registration requirements.

When can I start attending classes?

You can begin according to the schedule provided after your admission has been confirmed by the academy.

Inside a Training Session

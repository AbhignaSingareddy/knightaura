— WHO WE ARE

Every move has a purpose. Every learner has potential.

KnightAura is a chess learning space built around curiosity, practice, and the joy of improving.

We believe chess should be more than memorising moves. Students should understand why a move works, how to recognise opportunities, and when to rethink their strategy.

Our approach encourages learners to ask questions, practise regularly, learn from mistakes, and enjoy progress at their own pace.

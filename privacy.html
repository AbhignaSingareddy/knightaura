Privacy Policy

At Knight Aura Chess Academy, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how information shared through our website and academy enquiries may be collected, used and protected.

Your Information, Our Responsibility

Knight Aura Chess Academy values the trust of students, parents and visitors. This Privacy Policy describes how information you choose to share with us may be handled when you visit our website or enquire about our chess coaching programmes.

01. Information We Collect

When you contact us or enquire about our services, you may choose to provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, student details and coaching preferences.

Basic technical information may also be processed by the website or its hosting services to support security and normal operation.

02. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, explain coaching programmes, communicate class schedules, assist with enrolment requests and provide information about academy activities.

We may also use relevant technical information to maintain website security and troubleshoot technical issues.

03. Students and Children’s Privacy

Our academy may provide chess learning opportunities for children and young learners. Parents or legal guardians should submit children's personal information only when authorised to do so.

We aim to limit the information requested to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant enquiry or academy activity.

04. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with relevant service providers when needed to operate the website or respond to your request, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

External websites and services may have their own privacy policies, which should be reviewed separately.

05. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure. However, no internet-based transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06. Your Privacy Requests

If you have questions about information you have submitted, or wish to request a correction, please contact Knight Aura Chess Academy through the contact details provided on our website.

07. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, services or applicable requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page.

Trust is part of every great move. We aim to treat our students, parents and visitors with care and respect.
